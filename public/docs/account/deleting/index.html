Your account

Deleting your account

How to delete your account and everything in it, and what happens to shared songs when you do.

Delete Account & Data is at the very bottom of the Settings tab, below Sign Out, in red.

It does what it says. This page exists so you know exactly what you are agreeing to before you tap it.

Heads up

Deleting your account is permanent. Your songs, recordings, concepts and projects are removed, and there is no undo and no grace period. If you only want to stop using the app on this device, sign out instead.

What it asks you#

The app checks three times, in three different ways, because the action cannot be taken back.

  1. Are you sure? The alert above, which says plainly that it is irreversible.
  2. Confirm deletion. A second alert warning that your song data will be unrecoverable.
  3. Prove it is you. A sign-in sheet, because deleting an account is not something a borrowed phone should be able to do.

Only after all three does anything happen. Cancel is available at every step.

While it runs#

A full-screen progress view counts through the work: fetching your songs, deleting them one by one, cleaning up invitations, then your user data, then the account itself.

It asks you not to close the app while this happens, and that is worth honouring. Stopping halfway leaves some of it done.

What happens to shared songs#

Songs you own that have co-writers on them do not vanish from those co-writers. Each of them keeps a personal copy under a new title, so the work they did survives. They are told the collaboration ended.

Songs somebody else owns are unaffected. You simply stop being on them.

What is not deleted#

Your subscription is not a Lyric Genie account, it is an Apple one. Deleting your account here does not cancel it.

Cancel a subscription through the App Store, under your Apple ID, before or after deleting the account. See Plans and Pro.

Heads up

Lyric sheets you created stay reachable at their links unless you delete them first, because they are published pages rather than account data. If you have shared sheets you want taken down, delete them from each song before you delete the account. See Lyric sheets.
