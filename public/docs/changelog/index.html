What's new

What changed in each release of Lyric Genie, newest first.

Showing the iOS appweb app ·

Every page of these docs says which version of the app it was written against, at the foot of the page. This is where that number leads: what actually changed, newest first.

Both apps are logged here. Web app releases are headed Web and marked with a chip. The iOS log starts at 2.19. Anything older shipped before we kept one.

2.23#

October 2026

Type straight into the Master, record an idea without opening the app, and live sessions on iPhone.

The Master, editable#

Type in the Master. Tap into it and it locks to you: a lock in your colour shows on the Master tab, and everyone else sees your edits live. Tap the lock to let someone else in, or it frees up after 30 seconds without typing. Tap a Master someone else is editing and a note says who, and when it frees up. See The Master.

Send to Master just sends. Lines you send go to the end of Master, the way Send to Me works, with no questions about sections. Sending to the Master, or arranging it, waits while someone else holds the lock.

Arrange Master. On the Master tab the Arrange button reads Arrange Master, and the ✕ Arrange pill to leave Arrange mode now sits just above the Arrange bar. See Arrange mode.

Record an Idea#

"Hey Siri, record an idea in Lyric Genie." Recording starts straight away, even from the Lock Screen, with a timer and Stop in the Dynamic Island. Run it again to save the take to your Ideas. It also goes on the Action Button and as a Control Center or Lock Screen control. Needs iOS 18. See Siri and the Action Button.

Sessions on iPhone#

Join a session from your phone. Start or join one from the song's ⋯ menu, or the video button in the chat. A lobby lets you check your camera first, and the session carries on in a floating bubble and picture-in-picture while you write.

It behaves like a phone call. A session shows in the green pill and the Dynamic Island, with mute and end on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. It plays through AirPods and the car, and appears in CarPlay. A phone call puts it on hold. See Sessions.

Chat and notifications#

  • A push for each chat message, titled with who sent it, and one "new messages" item per song in Notifications that clears when you read the chat, on any device.
  • Lines sent to you push and count the same way. Their card in the chat has Add to my lyrics, the new Add to Master, and Decline.
  • News from Lyric Genie opens in its own sheet.

Also#

  • First launch has a Photo step after your name. See First launch.
  • Songs reopen on the tab you used last, on this device.
  • The selection bar now appears on collaborators' tabs and the Master, with the actions that do not change their words.
  • Tap a collaborator to see their profile. See Your profile.
  • Collaborators' cursors no longer run ahead of their words.
  • The private Scratchpad label uses an eye-slash icon. The lock now means Master editing.
  • The Pro screen checks for a web subscription before showing plans, so you cannot pay twice. See Plans and Pro.
  • Wish Workshop's Keep meter and in-line suggestions are more accurate.

Web 0.4 to 0.8 Web app only#

October 2026

The Master, editable#

Type in the Master. Click into it and it locks to you, with a lock in your colour on the Master tab. Click the lock to let others in, or it frees up after 30 seconds without typing. Others see your edits live, and a toast says who is editing if they try. Send to Master puts the lines at the end of Master, with no questions about sections. See The Master.

Chat and notifications#

  • Invitations pop up wherever you are, as a card with Accept or Join, and Decline. Pop-ups slide in at the top right.
  • Session started cards, with Join and Not now.
  • A push for each chat message, and one "new messages" item per song that clears when the chat is read.
  • Lines sent to you push too. Add to my lyrics opens Arrange with the new lines picked, and Add to Master is new.
  • Links in chat open in a new tab, messages can be selected and copied, and an emoji on its own shows large. A recording's title in chat opens it in Recordings.

See Collaborator chat and Notifications.

Sessions#

  • Just a session: start one from Home without a song.
  • The session follows you around the app in a floating pill, and can pop out into its own window. Room view lets it fill the page.
  • Speaker or grid view, tap to pin someone. A weak connection lowers the video quality instead of pausing it. Choose your camera and its quality.
  • Audio: send an interface's inputs 1–2 as a pair, or input 1 or 2 alone, or both as separate sources with + Input 2. Duck mic lowers your mic while your DAW plays. Turn a collaborator up to 150%. Computer audio no longer echoes the session.
  • Record everything, or one person's mic or input. Takes are named for where they came from, such as "Session · Sam's mic · 5:54 PM", and a RECORDING badge shows who is recording.
  • Keys: ⌘ D or M mutes your mic, ⇧ ⌘ D or V your camera. See Keyboard shortcuts.
  • A session ends for each person after 5 hours, with a warning first. A song can have up to 8 writers, counting open invites.
  • Refreshing the page no longer rejoins a session.

See Sessions.

Writing#

  • AI picks in the rhyme tool load by themselves, about three seconds after the rhymes show. See Rhymes.
  • Genie answers how-to questions from these docs, with links. See Genie chat.
  • Songs reopen on the tab you were last on, in this browser.
  • Wish Workshop follows edits to the words it is working on, and in-line suggestions pick lines nearer the length you need.
  • "Private" (the scratchpad) uses an eye icon. The lock now means Master editing.

Recordings#

  • Click the playing take to pause it.
  • Collaborators on a shared song can download your original files.

Account#

  • Lyric Sheets in Settings lists every sheet you have published, and search finds them. See The Settings tab.
  • The welcome for new accounts has an optional photo step.
  • Signing in from Gmail, Instagram and other apps' built-in browsers now explains how to open the page in your real browser. See Signing in.
  • "Co-writers" are now collaborators everywhere in the app.

2.22#

October 2026

The biggest iPhone update since launch: chat with your collaborators, Genie, and a new bar for the writing tools.

Writing together#

Collaborator chat. Talk in the song, with photos and recordings attached. Lines a collaborator sends you arrive in the chat instead of a separate Mailbox. See Collaborator chat.

See who is here. Collaborators' cursors and selections show live, in their colour, and profile photos show beside their names. See Your profile.

Notes pinned to words, with replies. See Notes on lyrics.

Genie on iPhone#

The Genie chat is in the app, the same chat as on the web, from the Genie button in the editor.

A docked bar for the writing tools#

The floating lamp and grid buttons, and the bar above the keyboard, are replaced by one bar along the bottom of the editor. With nothing selected it has Arrange, Insert Blank, Wish, Genie and Done. With text selected it becomes a sideways row of every action for the selection. See The song editor.

Also#

  • Alternate lines, written main / alternate. See Alternate lines.
  • Original files on Pro: imports kept exactly as uploaded. See Original files.
  • The free plan has 15 AI calls and 50 quick AI calls. See Plan limits.
  • Perform fills the whole screen.

2.21#

October 2026

Blanks#

Leave a gap and come back to it. Two underscores make a one-syllable blank, drawn as a line. Tap a blank to change its length, set a word it should rhyme with, and get suggestions that fill it exactly. Make Blank turns a selection into one, and Wish Workshop fills them too. See Blanks.

A private scratchpad#

On a shared song, each writer's Scratchpad is their own, and nobody else sees it.

Web 0.3 Web app only#

October 2026

Search everything#

One search for the whole library. The search box at the top of Home, or ⌘K (Ctrl K on Windows) anywhere, finds songs, lines on every writer's tab and the Master, notes, chat messages, recordings, ideas, concepts and projects, as you type. The search button in the top bar opens the same thing. See Search everything.

Also#

  • A session's comings and goings show in the song's chat, so you can see who joined and when.

Web 0.2 Web app only#

October 2026

Sessions, in early access#

See and hear your collaborators while you write. A session is live video and voice inside a song, with the lyrics still in front of everyone. Share your screen, record the session to the song's Recordings, and set how loud each audio input is sent. Sessions are a Pro feature and are open to a small group first. See Sessions.

Bring your DAW in. The Lyric Genie Session plugin sends your DAW's audio into the session, so collaborators hear the track itself. See Share your DAW live.

Home#

Quick start. Home opens with four ways in: Write, Record, Concept and Session, beside a greeting that changes each day. Your name beside it opens your profile.

Also#

  • A notification about a new recording opens Recordings at that take.
  • A shared song nobody has joined yet can be unshared and deleted in one step, with Stop Sharing & Delete in the song's menu.
  • News from Lyric Genie appears in the notifications bell.

Web 0.1 Web app only#

October 2026

The web app, at app.lyricgenie.app. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account you use on your iPhone and your whole library is there: songs, recordings, concepts and projects, with the writing tools, Wish Workshop, Genie, collaboration and lyric sheets. These arrived during its first week.

Pro on the web#

Subscribe in the browser. Upgrade in Settings buys Pro, yearly or monthly, with the first 14 days free for new subscribers. One subscription covers the web app and the iOS app.

Cancel in one click. Settings has Cancel subscription for a web subscription, and Resume if you change your mind before it ends. For an App Store subscription it links to Apple's steps.

Have a code? in the upgrade dialog takes a promo code.

See Plans and Pro.

Writing together#

Chat with your collaborators in the song's side panel, with a line that shows when someone is typing. Attach photos, or audio, which joins the song's Recordings when you send it. A recording's menu has Send to Chat. See Chat.

See who is here. Collaborators in the song show at the top, with what they are doing: writing, and in which section, recording, or typing in chat. Open their tab to see their cursor and selection in their colour.

Side by side. Right-click a writer's tab and choose Open side by side to read two tabs at once.

Also new: a private scratchpad on shared songs, Stop sharing, and joining a song with a code. See Collaborator tabs.

Writing#

  • Notes pinned to words, with replies, and resolve when you are done. See Notes on lyrics.
  • Alternate lines. Write main line / other version and the alternate is shown faded, ready to swap in. The rhyme tools and the AI read the main line. See Alternate lines.
  • Refine beside AI Context offers options for the mood, theme and artists. See The song wizard.
  • Duplicate a song as a new one.
  • Typing a chorus header that already has words offers the earlier chorus.

Recordings#

  • Original files on Pro: imported audio is also kept exactly as you uploaded it, ready to download. Original Files in Settings lists them. See Plans and Pro.
  • Uploading audio is now called Upload, and a player has its own volume.

Plans#

  • The free plan now has 15 AI calls and 50 quick AI calls. In-line suggestions and rhyme picks only use quick calls, so they never spend the AI calls you keep for the tools you choose. Automatic retries are not counted. See Plan limits.

Also#

  • Profile photo. Upload one and position it, or use your Google photo. Collaborators see changes straight away.
  • Editing the same song on two computers no longer loses work. Edits merge, and if two edits clash on the same line, yours is kept as a separate copy of the song.

2.20#

September 2026

Waveforms that show up wherever the recording goes, a fix for long lyrics, and some care for iPad.

Waveforms#

They show up on every device. Each recording now carries its own waveform, so it appears straight away on a collaborator's phone, your iPad or a new phone, instead of sitting blank until you press play and download the audio.

Long recordings are no longer heavy to draw. Working out a waveform used to hold the whole recording in memory at once — over 2 GB for an hour-long file — which could stall or close the app. It now takes the same small amount however long the audio is.

iPad#

  • Recordings draw their waveform in twice the detail across the wider screen.
  • The Record, Import and Freestyle sheets draw bars the same size as on iPhone, rather than cramming or stretching them to fit.
  • The Record sheet no longer cuts the record button off at the bottom.

Fixed#

  • With very long lyrics, the bottom of the song could be cut off after inserting a transcription or pasting a large block of text. The editor now always makes room for all of it.

2.19#

September 2026

Projects have colours, the Action Button can start a take without you opening the app, and a few things that used to leave a mess behind no longer do.

Projects have colours#

Name it and colour it in one sheet. Creating a project now asks for both, and editing one opens the same sheet with the name and colour filled in. The palette is the same seven that concepts use, and each new project is offered the next colour along, so a list of them varies without you choosing.

Songs say which folders they are in. A line above the song title names every project the song is filed in, each in its own colour. It reads the same wherever you see the song, including inside a folder.

Tap a folder name to go to that folder. The rest of the row still opens the song.

See Projects.

Start writing without opening the app#

Three shortcuts. Start a recording, start a Freestyle, or open a blank song. Put any of them on the Action Button, ask Siri, or drop them into a Shortcut. A recording starts the moment the app is up, so the idea is captured while you are still reaching for it.

Concepts#

Colours without choosing them. Every new concept takes the next colour along, and a generated batch walks the palette, so a full tab reads at a glance instead of as a wall of one colour.

A rebuilt Generate Concepts sheet, matching the rest of the app rather than looking like a form bolted on. The switches now say why they are unavailable when they are.

See Concepts.

Recordings#

Search when filing a recording. Adding a recording to a song used to mean scrolling your whole library. There is a search field on that sheet now.

Archived songs are no longer offered as somewhere to file a recording.

Fixed#

  • Backing out of a new song, or cancelling a Freestyle, no longer leaves an empty song called "My Song" behind.
  • The project colour picker stretched the sheet wider than the screen on every iPhone except the Pro Max. It now fits any width.
  • Freestyle's Undo uses the same backspace icon as the one in Record, rather than a different glyph for the same job.
  • Freestyle's Import adds a file to the end of the take rather than being described as though it replaced it. See Freestyle.

Note

These docs arrived with this release too, at lyricgenie.app/docs. Every screen in the app, written up with screenshots, and searchable.
