Collaboration

Sending lines to a co-writer

Move lyrics between writers on a shared song. Nothing arrives in someone's tab without them accepting it.

Your tab is yours alone, so getting a line from your tab into a co-writer's means sending it. They decide whether to take it.

That is the whole design. Nothing lands in anybody's lyrics without them agreeing to it.

Sending#

Open Arrange mode, the grid button at the bottom right, and select the lines you want. Tapping a section header takes the whole stanza.

Send to Collaborator asks who should get them.

On someone else's tab the action changes to Send to Me, which pulls their lines into your own tab instead. Same idea, other direction.

The mailbox#

Lines you are sent wait in the mailbox, the button in the editor toolbar. It carries a count when something is waiting.

Each card shows who sent it, which song it belongs to, when it arrived, and the lyrics themselves.

  • Accept adds them to your tab.
  • Decline turns them down, and the sender sees that it was declined.

Keeping track of what you sent#

The Sent tab lists everything you have sent and what happened to it.

Status What it means
Pending They have not looked at it yet.
Accepted It is in their tab.
Declined They passed.
Expired It sat too long.

Resend puts a declined or expired set back in front of them, and lets you pick a different recipient.

Tip

Clear All empties the Received list. It does not ask first, and it does not delete anything from the song, only the notifications about it.

Sending to the agreed version instead#

Send to Master skips the mailbox entirely and puts the lines straight into the shared version. Nobody has to accept it, because Master is the song rather than anyone's personal tab. See The Master.
