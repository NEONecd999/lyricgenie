Collaboration

Song sessions

See and hear your collaborators live while you write a song together, on the web or on your iPhone. Share your DAW or computer audio from a computer, and record the session to the song.

Showing the iOS appweb app ·

A session is a live video call inside a song. Everyone in it sees and hears each other while the lyrics stay on the page, so you can talk a line through, play a part, or listen to a demo together without a separate call app.

Sessions are for the people writing the song. Only the song's writers can join its session, and each song has one session at a time. They work in the web app and in the iPhone app from version 2.23.

ProPro plan

Starting a session is part of Pro, and a free trial counts. Joining is free: while a Pro writer is in the session, any writer on the song can join, and everyone in it can share audio and record. Without Pro, you can join when a collaborator is in a session, and starting one offers Start a free trial. If every Pro writer has left, new writers without Pro can't join until one is back.

Starting a session#

Tip

Headphones help. Without them your speakers can feed back into your mic. See There is an echo.

From Home#

Click the green Session circle in Home's quick start. It opens Start a session, which has three parts:

  • The camera and mic check. A preview of your camera, a level meter for your microphone, and menus to choose which Microphone and Camera to use. The two round buttons on the preview turn your mic and camera on or off before you go in.
  • What the session is for. Just a session is picked to begin with: a call with nothing to set up. See Just a session. Choose Session with a song to write while you talk. It starts a New song: type a title, or leave it and the song is called "Session" with today's date. Choose an existing song instead lets you search your library and pick one. If a collaborator is already in a session on one of your songs, that song is picked for you and marked Live now.
  • Invite collaborators. Optional. See Inviting people.

Click Start session when you are ready. If a collaborator is already in it, the button reads Join session.

Inside a song#

On a shared song, Session is a section of the left panel, between Collaborators and Chat. It also has an icon in the column of icons on the left edge of the window.

Click Start a session in that section. A smaller check opens with just your camera and mic, and Start session.

The check has a box underneath: Skip this check next time (join with these settings). Tick it and later sessions in this browser start straight away, with the same mic and camera choices. To check them again, click Check camera and mic first under the start button.

A solo song has no Session section. Start the session from Home and choose the song there, or invite someone first.

From the Start tab#

Tap the Session circle. Start a session shows a preview of your camera, with buttons to join muted or with your camera off, and asks What's it for?:

  • Just a session. Talk now. It becomes a song once someone writes or names it. See Just a session.
  • A new song. Give it a title, and write it together.
  • One of my songs. Choose a song from your library.

Tap Start session.

Inside a song#

On a shared song, tap ⋯ and choose Live Session. The Session button at the top of the song's chat does the same. The session starts straight away, without a check.

The first time, the app asks for the camera and microphone. Say no to either and the session carries on without it. You can change that later in the iOS Settings app, under Lyric Genie.

A session needs a shared song. Starting one on a new song, or on a song that is just yours so far, shares it at that moment. The song then grows a Collaborators list and writer tabs, the same as any shared song.

Just a session#

Sometimes you only want to talk. Just a session is a call with no song to set up first.

Behind it there is a song, but it stays out of your Songs list, search and everything else while the call is only talk. It becomes an ordinary shared song as soon as someone writes in it, gives it a name, or chooses to start writing.

The session fills the page, with Collaborators, Recordings and Chat in a column beside the video. On a phone they are tabs under the video: Chat, People and Recordings.

Click Start writing to bring the lyrics up. Once someone else has started, the button reads Open the lyrics.

The Song pill at the top of the session opens the song, so you can start writing.

If the session ends with nothing written, the song goes away. Any takes recorded in it are kept: each person's move to their own Ideas.

Inviting people#

There is room for eight collaborators on a song, you included, and invites nobody has used yet count. See Inviting a collaborator.

On Start a session, Invite collaborators gives you two ways to bring people in:

  • Email. People you have written songs with before are listed with their email and how many songs you share. Tick the ones you want. When you click Start session, they get an email saying you are starting a session, with a link to join.
  • Code. New collaborator makes an invite code to send to anyone, however you like. Each code works once, so make one per person with Another new collaborator. Whoever uses a code joins the song as a collaborator, and goes straight to the session's camera and mic check.

While you are alone in a session, a Waiting for collaborators strip shows who you invited, and Copy invite link copies a link to send.

Inside a song, invite people the usual way, from the Collaborators section.

In a session, tap Invite someone at the top. On a song it opens the song's Collaborators list, where you can invite people the usual way. In Just a session it shares a link to the session with any app you like.

Joining a session#

When a collaborator starts a session, the song's other writers are told: Sam started a session. They are told at most once every half hour per song.

  • A card slides in at the top right, wherever you are in the app: Sam is in a session on the song, with Join and Not now.
  • The bell has a started a session notification. Clicking it opens the camera and mic check for that song, then takes you in.
  • The song. If you open a song while collaborators are in its session, a green banner over the lyrics says who, for example Sam is in a session on this song. Click Join. The × hides the banner for this visit. The Session section and its icon on the left also show a green Live dot.
  • Home. The Session circle shows that a collaborator is live on one of your recent shared songs.

An invitation to a session also pops up as a card, Sam invited you to a session on the song, with Join and Decline.

  • A push notification, even when the app is closed. Tap it to join.
  • The song. A banner at the bottom says who is in the session, with Join. The Chat button in the toolbar turns into a video button with a green dot while a session is going, red while it is being recorded.
  • An invitation to a session shows as a note at the top of the screen, Sam invited you to a session, with Join.

If everyone has left by the time you tap, the app says Nobody's in this session right now and offers to start a new one.

The song's chat marks the moment each person joins or leaves, as a small line such as Sam joined the session with the time.

The session on the web#

In the left panel#

While you are in a session, the Session section shows everyone's video, the Audio inputs list, and a row of round buttons. A coloured ring around a video means that person is talking.

Drag the bar under the video to make it taller or shorter. Double-click the bar to put it back.

Button What it does
Microphone Mutes and unmutes you. Purple when on.
Camera Turns your camera off and on. Purple when on. The arrow beside it picks the camera and its quality.
Headphones Music mode. See below.
Sliders Adds an audio input. See Audio inputs.
Screen Shares your screen. See Sharing your screen.
Record Records the session. The arrow beside it picks what to record. See Recording a session.
Pop out Puts the session in its own window. See Pop out.
Leave Leaves the session.

Keyboard shortcuts#

While you are in a session:

Keys What it does
⌘ D (Ctrl D on Windows), or M when you are not typing Mutes and unmutes your mic.
⌘ Shift D (Ctrl Shift D), or V when you are not typing Turns your camera off and on.

A brief note says Mic muted, Mic on, Camera off or Camera on.

Speaker and grid#

With more than one video, a switch in the corner of the video picks how they are laid out:

  • Speaker view shows whoever is talking, large, with the others small. It is the starting view on a phone.
  • Grid view shows everyone the same size. It is the starting view on a computer.

Click any video to pin it large. Unpin lets the view follow the talking again. The app remembers your choice on this device.

The expanded view#

The expand button on the Session header, or the Session icon on the left edge, opens the session at full size, with the videos as large as the space allows.

The chat button in the Session header shows the song's chat as a column beside the video. It starts hidden, and the app remembers your choice. A red dot on it means unread messages. Drag the line between the video and the chat to resize them, and double-click it to reset.

Room view#

Room view, in the Session section's header, lets the session fill the whole page, with Collaborators, Recordings and Chat in a column beside it. It changes only what you see, not what anyone else sees.

Lyrics at the top goes back to the song. It lights up when the lyrics have changed while you were in the room. When the column is closed, a small pill shows how many people are in the call, how many takes there are, and unread messages.

Around the app#

The session keeps going when you leave the song for another page. A purple pill floats at the bottom of the window: In a session and the song's title, with your mic button, Return to go back to the song, and the red button to leave. Recordings still save to the session's song wherever you are.

Refreshing the page does not rejoin a session you have left. It just opens the song.

Your camera#

The arrow beside the camera button opens Camera and quality:

  • Camera lists the cameras on this computer. Pick one and it switches straight away.
  • Camera quality is what your camera sends: Data saver, Standard (the starting setting) or High. Lower it if your video freezes for others.

Both are remembered on this device.

Sharing your screen#

Click the screen button and your browser asks what to share: a window, a tab, or the whole screen. Everyone sees it as its own tile, next to your camera. When a collaborator starts sharing, their screen comes up large. Click the button again to stop.

Screen sharing works on a computer. Phones and tablets cannot share a screen from the browser.

Pop out#

In Chrome and Edge on a computer, the pop-out button moves the session into a small window that stays on top of other apps. That keeps your collaborators in view while you work in a DAW. The button turns purple, and the panel says Session is in its own window. Click Bring back, or the pop-out button again, to return it. In other browsers the button is not there.

On a phone#

In a phone's browser the video sits at a fixed height above the song, in speaker view, with the buttons on one row. Audio inputs starts folded away. Tap a video to open the full session view.

The session on the iPhone#

The session opens over the song. At the top: the song's title, how many people are in it, Invite someone, and a Lyrics pill to go to the song and write. (In Just a session the pill reads Song.) The chevron at the top left minimises it.

Along the bottom:

Button What it does
Microphone Mutes and unmutes you.
Camera Turns your camera off and on.
Flip Switches between the front and back camera.
Music mode For singing or playing into your mic. See Music mode.
Record Records the session. See Recording a session.
Red phone Leaves the session.

Under the video, Chat | Levels switches between the song's chat and the levels of everyone's audio. A red number on Chat counts unread messages. The chevron hides the panel to give the video more room.

Levels has a slider and a mute for each person. Your own mic's slider is how loud you are for everyone. A collaborator's slider and mute are just for you.

While you write#

Minimise the session and it shrinks to a bubble at the bottom right, showing whoever is talking, with a mute button. Drag it out of the way, or tap the × to shrink it to a small Live pill. Tap it to open the session again.

Leave the app and the bubble becomes picture in picture, floating over other apps, and the audio keeps going in the background.

It works like a phone call#

The iPhone treats a session like a call:

  • The green pill, or the Dynamic Island, shows you are in a session. Tap it to come back to the app.
  • The lock screen and Control Center have mute and end buttons, the same as for a phone call. Muting there mutes you in the session, and ending there leaves it.
  • Sound goes where calls go: to AirPods, a headset, or your car. In a car with CarPlay, the session shows on the call screen, titled with the song.
  • A phone call puts the session on hold. It picks up again when you hang up.
  • Nothing rings. Sessions are not phone calls to anybody. People join when they choose to, as with Zoom or Google Meet, and sessions do not appear in the Phone app's recents.

On an iPhone set to the China region, sessions work without the call features.

What needs a computer#

From an iPhone you cannot share your DAW, computer audio or your screen. You still see a collaborator's shared screen, and hear audio they share. To share yours, join the session from the web app on a Mac or PC. See Share your DAW live.

Music mode#

Music mode is for singing or playing an instrument into your mic. It turns off echo cancellation and noise reduction so the sound comes through as it is. The app asks you to put on headphones first, because without them your speakers feed straight back into your mic, and your collaborators hear themselves. Turn it off again the same way.

It is the headphones button.

It is the music mode button along the bottom of the session. Tap Turn on to confirm.

Audio inputs#

Audio inputs lists every sound source in the session, each with a live meter. Your own come first, then your collaborators'. Click the heading to fold it away.

Your microphone#

The first row is your mic, with the device's name. The small arrow beside it switches to another microphone.

Your mic has echo cancellation and noise reduction, which suits talking.

Adding audio#

Add audio input, under your rows, or the sliders button, opens a menu of other things you can share. Anything shared this way goes out clearly, without the voice processing your mic gets.

  • Your DAW. Live audio from your DAW through the Lyric Genie Session plugin. See Share your DAW live.
  • Computer audio (a tab or your screen). Your browser asks what to share, starting on the whole screen, where Chrome's Share system audio switch is. In Chrome and Edge, turn on the audio switch in that window, or no sound comes through. The session's own sound is left out, so collaborators do not hear themselves back. Safari cannot share computer audio this way. Use an audio device instead.
  • Audio devices. An audio interface, or a virtual device such as BlackHole or Loopback that your DAW plays into. Your current microphone is left off this list, so you are never heard twice.

You can share up to three audio inputs at once, as well as your mic. Remove one to add another.

Inputs 1 and 2 of an interface#

A browser gets the first two inputs of an audio interface. The 1–2 / 1 / 2 switch on the device's row says what to send:

  • 1–2 sends them as a stereo pair. This is where it starts.
  • 1 or 2 sends just that input, in mono.

It switches live, and is remembered for each device.

With one input sent alone, + Input 2 (or + Input 1) on the row adds the other one as a source of its own. That way a vocal mic on input 1 and a guitar on input 2 go out as two sources, each with its own volume and mute. Collaborators see them as the device's name and Input 1 or Input 2.

Levels#

Each row has a meter, a volume slider and a mute button. What they do depends on whose row it is.

Row Slider Mute
Yours How loud it is for everyone. 100% sends it as it is; you can go up to 150% for a quiet source. Mutes it for everyone. It stays shared.
A collaborator's How loud you hear it, up to 150%. Only you. Mutes it for you only.

When a collaborator has muted their own mic or input, their row says Muted.

Point at a slider to see its percentage. Your own extra inputs also have an × to stop sharing them.

The meter is green, turns yellow in the last 3 dB, and shows red when a peak reaches the top. If your source keeps hitting red, turn its slider down.

If you play your DAW through speakers, Duck mic lowers your mic while the track plays, and your mic shows Ducked. See Duck mic.

Recording a session#

The record button records the session. Everything is recorded as you hear it: everyone's mics and every shared source, mixed together, up to 60 minutes.

Tap the record button and confirm with Record.

The arrow beside the record button chooses What to record:

  • Everything, the whole mix as you hear it. This is where it starts.
  • One source: your mic, your DAW, or a collaborator's mic or input, on its own. You can change it while recording.

While it records, the button turns red and shows the time. Click it to stop. Saved · Show takes you to the take.

The recording saves to the song's Recordings as Session recording followed by the time it started. A recording of one source is titled with it, such as Session · Sam's mic · 5:54 PM. Your collaborators get the usual notification that a recording was added.

Everyone in the session is told when recording starts and stops, whoever starts it. They hear a chime, see a short notice, and a red RECORDING badge stays on the session saying who is recording, such as RECORDING (by Sam). Joining a session that is already being recorded tells you so.

In the last five minutes you are warned, and the recording stops and saves itself at the limit. Leaving the session while recording also saves what you have.

Connection quality#

If a connection is weak, the session lowers the quality of the video coming in so the audio stays smooth, and brings full quality back by itself once the connection recovers. A collaborator with a weak connection shows a Weak label on their video.

If the connection drops, the session shows Reconnecting… and carries on when it can.

If your own video freezes for others, lower Camera quality in the menu beside the camera button.

Leaving and ending#

Click Leave to leave. Closing the tab also takes you out of the session.

Tap the red phone to leave, or end the call from the lock screen or Control Center.

A session ends when the last person leaves. Nothing about the song changes, and your collaborators can start a new one at any time.

So a session left open by mistake does not run on for hours, the app ends it for you:

  • Alone. If you are the only one in the session for 10 minutes, you leave it, with a note saying why.
  • Quiet. After about an hour and a half with nobody talking, nothing shared, and no typing or tapping, the app asks Still in the session? and counts down a minute. Choose Stay to keep going, or leave.
  • Long. After about four hours the same question appears, however busy the session is.
  • Five hours. Each person can stay in a session for up to five hours at a time. You are warned in the last ten minutes, and then you leave it. A recording in progress is saved. You can rejoin straight away.

Talking, playing, or sharing audio counts as staying, so a session with music playing from your DAW is not cut off while you work in another app.
