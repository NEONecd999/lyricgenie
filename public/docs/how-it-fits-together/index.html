How it fits together

Five tabs, four kinds of object, and the relationships between them.

Lyric Genie has five tabs. Four of them hold a different kind of object, and the fifth is Settings.

Each one has a page of its own. Start with whichever tab you are standing in.

Tab Holds What it is
Start nothing of its own The launch pad. Jump straight into writing or recording.
Songs Songs The documents you write lyrics in.
Concepts Concepts Ideas you have not turned into songs yet.
Recordings Recordings Every piece of audio you have captured, in one list.
Settings — Account, plan, preferences.

The rest of this page is the part the tab pages do not cover: how the things in them relate to each other.

The one relationship that matters#

A Recording belongs to a Song. Not to the Recordings tab.

The Recordings tab is a view across all your audio, the way a photo library is a view across photos that also live in albums. When you record from inside a song, the audio is attached to that song and also appears in the Recordings list. When you record from the Start tab, there is no song yet, so the audio lands in the Recordings list unattached until you assign it.

Note

This is why the Recordings tab has an Add to Song action and the song editor does not. From the song's side, the recording is already there.

That single fact explains most of the "where did my recording go" confusion. If you recorded something and cannot find it in a song, it is almost certainly sitting unattached in Recordings, waiting to be assigned.

The rest of the shape#

Concepts sit upstream of songs. A concept is a title, a mood, a theme, and optionally some similar artists. It is the note you make when you have the idea but not the song. When you are ready, a concept becomes a song.

Projects sit above songs. A project is a named, colour-tagged folder that holds a list of songs. An album, an artist you write for, a session. A song can be in several projects at once, or in none.

Collaborators sit inside a song. Each co-writer gets their own tab and their own colour, so you can see who wrote what, and there is a Master tab where the agreed version gets assembled.

So, reading from smallest to largest:

Concept ──becomes──▶ Song ──grouped into──▶ Project
                      │
                      ├── Lyrics (yours, plus a tab per collaborator, plus Master)
                      └── Recordings ──also listed in──▶ the Recordings tab

Where your work is stored#

Everything syncs to your account, so the same songs and recordings appear on any device you sign in on. Signing in is what makes that work. If you are not signed in, you are not syncing.
