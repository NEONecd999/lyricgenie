The five tabs

The Chats tab

Every chat you are in, in one list. Your shared songs' chats, chats with collaborators that are not a song yet, unread counts, and archiving.

Showing the iOS appweb app ·

Chats collects every chat you are in. That means the chat of each shared song, and chats with collaborators that have no song yet. Use it to catch up on what your collaborators said without opening each song.

What happens inside a chat, from sending recordings to lines a collaborator sends you, is on Collaborator chat.

Finding Chats#

Chats is the last tab in the bar: Start, Songs, Recordings, Concepts, Chats. A red number on the tab counts the chats with messages you have not read.

Chats took the place of the Settings tab. Settings now opens from your profile picture. See Settings.

On a computer, Chats is the speech bubbles icon in the top bar, beside the bell. On a phone it is the last tab in the bar along the bottom. Either way, a red number counts the chats with messages you have not read.

Press G then M to go to Chats from anywhere.

Note

The Chats tab needs version 2.30 or later of the iPhone app.

The list#

Each row is one chat: its name, who is in it, the last message and when it was sent. A chat with unread messages shows in bold, with a count on the right. Chats with the newest messages come first.

A chat that belongs to a song is named after the song, and carries the Songs icon. A chat with no song is named after the people in it, such as Sam & Alex, until someone gives it a name.

Tap a row to open the chat at full height. Song › at the top right opens the song. Back from the song returns to the chat.

Tap the chat's name at the top to see who is in it, whether they have read the latest messages, and to start a session on the song.

  • Swipe right on a row to mark it read, or unread again.
  • Swipe left for Archive.
  • Touch and hold a row for Open Song, Start Session, Mark as Read or Mark as Unread, and Archive.

The search box at the top looks through chat names, people and each chat's last message. The sort button orders the list by Recent Activity or Name. The ⋯ button has the filter (All, Unread, Songs or Chats without a song), Mark All as Read and Show Archive.

Click a chat to open it. On a computer the list stays on the left and the chat fills the right. On a phone the chat opens full screen, and the arrow at the top left goes back to the list.

The top of the chat shows its name and how many people are in it. Click the photos to see who is in it. Open song opens the song.

Search chats looks through chat names, people and each chat's last message. All and Unread filter the list.

Starting a chat without a song#

Sometimes you want to talk an idea through before there is a song. A new chat works like a song's chat, with recordings and photos, and becomes a song when you are ready.

Tap the New Chat button (a speech bubble with a plus) at the top right.

  1. Pick collaborators. Everyone you have written a song with is listed. Search by name or email.
  2. Invite someone new holds a place for anyone else. The chat opens with its invite link ready to share.
  3. Give it a name under Chat Title if you like. Without one, it is named after who is in it.
  4. Tap Start Chat.

If you already have a chat with exactly those people that is not a song yet, Lyric Genie asks whether to Open it or Start a new one.

With no chats yet, the tab explains the ways in, and Start a Chat does the same as New Chat.

Click New Chat.

  1. Pick collaborators. Everyone you have written a song with is listed.
  2. Give it a name if you like. Without one, it is named after who is in it.
  3. Click Start Chat.

If you already have a chat with just those people, the button says Open Chat and takes you there instead.

A chat holds up to eight people, you included.

A chat's people#

Tap the chat's name at the top. For a chat with no song, this is also where you rename it under Chat Title, add people you have written with, share its invite link, and Leave Chat. Done saves the name.

Click the photos at the top of the chat, or choose People from the ⋯ menu. For a chat with no song, this is also where you rename it, add people you have written with, share its invite link, and Leave Chat.

A song's chat has the song's collaborators in it. To add someone, invite them to the song. See Inviting a collaborator.

Inviting someone new#

A chat with no song has one invite link. Share it with anyone you want in the chat. Anyone with the link can join the chat and read it, until you reset the link or turn it off from its ⋯ menu. A reset link replaces the old one, which stops working.

Note

Someone joining from a chat link on an iPhone needs version 2.30 or later.

Leaving#

Leave Chat asks first. You stop seeing its messages. Someone still in the chat can add you back.

Turning a chat into a song#

Start Song at the top right of a chat with no song turns it into a song for everyone in it. The chat, its name and its recordings come along. The song appears in everyone's Songs and opens for you.

The web app asks first.

Archiving a chat#

There is no delete. Archive a chat to move it out of the list.

Swipe left on a row and tap Archive, or touch and hold it. Show Archive in the ⋯ menu lists archived chats, where Unarchive brings one back.

Choose Archive from the chat's ⋯ menu. The Archive button beside the filter shows archived chats in place of the list. Open one and choose Unarchive from its ⋯ menu. Back to Chats leaves the archive.

  • An archived chat comes back by itself as soon as someone else writes in it.
  • Archiving a song archives its chat too. It stays out of the way until a new message arrives. Unarchiving that chat unarchives the song as well.
  • Archiving a chat leaves its song alone.

Sharing into a chat from another app#

Send a photo, a piece of audio or some text from any app to a chat, through the share sheet.

  • Recent chats appear in the share sheet's top row. Pick one and that chat opens with the item in its message box.
  • Lyric Genie in the row of apps asks Where should this go? Pick a chat. For audio you can also choose Import as Recording, the usual import. With no chats yet, audio is imported straight away.

Nothing is sent automatically. The item waits in the message box, and you add a message if you like and tap send. Text arrives as a draft you can edit.

Chats show up in the share sheet once you have sent a message in them.
