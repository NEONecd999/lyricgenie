Questions about rhymes for “them”

Stem, chem, gem, hem, clem and phlegm rhyme with them. Longer rhymes include mayhem, alem, diadem and condemn. Phrases that end on the same sound include “see em”, “get em” and “need em”.
How many syllables are in them?
Them has 1 syllable. The rhyme falls on the sound “-em”.
What are near rhymes for them?
Near (slant) rhymes share the vowel sound but not every consonant: their, there, when, web, help and get. Songs use them all the time.
